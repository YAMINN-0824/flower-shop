Petunia

What is their meaning?

petunias symbolize a variety of meanings depending on their color. Generally, they represent feelings of anger and resentment, but they can also convey a sense of calm and peace. White petunias often symbolize purity and sincerity, while purple ones can represent a desire for connection. Overall, petunias are versatile flowers that can express a range of emotions, from love and admiration to deeper sentiments of longing or regret.
