Hydrangea

What is their meaning?

These are interesting because they symbolize many emotions. The blue hydrangea is especially linked to sorrow and sadness. The meaning of hydrangeas changes with their color. White hydrangeas can represent grace and purity, but they can also suggest pride or vanity in certain situations. Pink hydrangeas show warmth and are often connected to love and romance. Purple hydrangeas symbolize understanding and gratitude. Each color tells a different story, showing how these flowers express complex feelings like sorrow and hope, especially in green varieties.
